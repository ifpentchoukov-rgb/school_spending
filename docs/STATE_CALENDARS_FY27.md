# State Budget Adoption Calendars — FY27 (SY 2026-27)

_Last updated: 2026-05-06. Generated from the live `state_calendars` table; source of truth is the database._

This document lists the legal adoption deadline for the FY27 (school year 2026-27) operating budget for each US state + DC. The daily extractor runner (`runner/daily.py`) uses these dates to gate which states are actively adopting (and thus worth scraping for adopted budgets) on any given day.

**FY convention:** Most states run July 1 – June 30. Five states have non-standard fiscal years: TX (Sept-Aug), WA (Sept-Aug), AL (Oct-Sept since 2010), DC (federal Oct-Sept), IN (school operating moved to July-June in PL 217-2017). HI's biennial budget is set by the Legislature, not by district boards.

## Quirks and data notes

- **HI is biennial.** FY27 was set in 2025. There is no per-district adoption event for FY27 — HIDOE's allocation is set by the Legislature on a 2-year cycle.
- **TX, WA fiscal year is Sept-Aug** (not Jul-Jun). Adoption deadlines are pre-FY-start.
- **AL fiscal year is Oct-Sept** (changed in 2010 by Act 2010-528). The seed data has a `DATA NOTE` flagging that `master_districts.csv` has the wrong `fy_calendar` value for AL — verifier should reconcile.
- **DC fiscal year is federal Oct-Sept** (not July-June). Two LEAs in master: DCPS (state-administered) and PCSB-authorized charters. Council adoption + Congressional review.
- **IN moved school operating to July-June in 2017** (PL 217-2017). The `adoption_deadline` of 2026-11-01 reflects the property-tax cycle, not the operating-FY-start. The actual operating budget for FY27 was adopted in fall 2025 / approved by DLGF Feb 2026.
- **NY excludes Big 5 cities** (NYC, Buffalo, Rochester, Syracuse, Yonkers) from the May 19 voter referendum cycle — those have mayoral/council-adopted budgets on different timelines.
- **MT enrollment in master is ~21k**, far below actual ~150k. The seed note flags this as a master-data gap; many small MT districts are filtered out by the `is_operating_district` criterion.
- **OH is two-stage**: tax budget filed with county auditor by Jan 15, permanent appropriation by Oct 1 (temporary by April 1 acceptable). Coverage in our calendar uses the permanent-appropriation date.
- **OK is two-stage**: districts adopt by Aug 1, county excise board certifies by Oct 1. Districts may operate on temporary appropriation between July 1 FY start and certification.
- **KY is three-stage**: tentative by Jan 31 of preceding year, working by May 30, final by Sept 30. Calendar lists final.
- **NJ districts on April school election cycle** hold a voter referendum; most have moved to November general or no-vote (board-adopted) cycles since 2012. Type I districts (board-appointed) have no voter approval at all.

## How the runner uses this

`runner/daily.py` queries `state_calendars` each day and gates "active" states based on the proposed window:

```python
# pseudocode
for spec in REGISTRY.by_kind('budget'):
    cal = state_calendars[spec.state_postal][fiscal_year]
    if cal.proposed_window_start <= today <= cal.adoption_deadline + 30 days:
        run extractor
```

This means a state's budget extractor is dormant outside its adoption window and active during the proposed → 30 days post-adoption window. Actuals extractors (`kind=actuals`) are not gated by this calendar — they run on a separate cadence based on each state's audit-publication schedule.
